Terms of Use

Year 5 Knowledge Quest is an educational practice game for Year 5 / Key Stage 2 learners. It is intended to support learning and revision, not replace a school curriculum, teacher assessment or professional educational advice.

Educational content

Questions, explanations, interface text and game mechanics in this build are original. The game is organised to cover the English, Maths and Science topics selected for the project. Publisher scans, logos and copied exercise banks are not included.

Acceptable use

Users may play the game for personal and classroom learning. A public operator should not remove safety or privacy notices, misrepresent the game as an official product of a textbook publisher, or upload copyrighted textbook pages without permission.

Availability

The game may be updated as content is reviewed and expanded. Progress stored in a browser can be lost when browser data is cleared or a different device is used.
