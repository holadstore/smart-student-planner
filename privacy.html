Privacy-first public edition

Privacy

Year 5 Knowledge Quest is designed so a child can play without creating an account or publishing a profile.

What is stored

The game stores the chosen nickname, avatar, XP, coins, stars, completed sections, streaks and daily challenge progress in the browser's local storage on the device being used.

What is not collected by this build

  • No email address or phone number is required.
  • No real name is required.
  • No chat, messaging or public profile is provided.
  • No location, camera or microphone permission is requested.
  • No advertising or behavioural analytics scripts are included in this package.

Deleting progress

Progress can be cleared from Settings inside the game, or by clearing site data in the browser.

For site operators

If a future public deployment adds analytics, sign-in, cloud saves, payments, advertising or third-party services, this privacy notice must be updated before those features are enabled.

Child-safety principle: keep data collection to the minimum needed for learning, and do not add public communication features without a separate safeguarding and moderation design.
