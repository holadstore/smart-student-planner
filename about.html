About Year 5 Knowledge Quest

Year 5 Knowledge Quest turns English, Maths and Science revision into an adventure. Learners explore three subject worlds, study short explanations, answer challenges, collect XP and badges, and track mastery by section.

How learning works

  • Learn: read a concise lesson linked to the selected section.
  • Explain: use a prompt to put the idea into your own words.
  • Play: answer a short challenge with hints and explanations.
  • Master: complete sections, unlock rewards and take world challenges.

Designed for public use

The public edition does not require accounts, public leaderboards, chat or personal contact details. It works as a static web app and can be installed as a PWA on supported devices.

Content approach

The learning map was developed from the English, Maths and Science source books supplied for this project. Public-facing explanations and questions are independently written so the game can teach the same topic coverage without republishing textbook pages.
