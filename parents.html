Parents & Teachers

The game is designed to make practice enjoyable while keeping the adult view simple.

Recommended use

  • Choose one subject world and one mission at a time.
  • Ask the learner to complete the Explain-It prompt before the quiz.
  • Use wrong-answer explanations as teaching moments rather than penalties.
  • Mix quick challenges with slower learning missions.
  • Review the in-game Parent / Teacher progress screen to see mastered sections.

Progress

Progress is saved only on the current device in this edition. That makes the public build privacy-friendly, but it also means progress will not automatically follow a learner to another device.

Question bank

The game engine supports a larger question bank for each section. Public operators should expand and quality-check questions before using the game for formal assessment.

Best use: revision, practice and confidence building. Keep formal assessment separate unless the question bank has been independently reviewed for that purpose.
